## 2. Detailed Description

```markdown
crumbs — every piece you love.

crumbs is a minimal, aesthetic visual bookmarking app and moodboard for the pieces, objects, and fashion you adore across the internet.

With the crumbs Chrome extension, you can send any product from any website straight into your crumbs board with a single click.

KEY FEATURES
• One-Click Product Capture: Discover an item you love on any online store or magazine? Click the crumbs extension to save it instantly.
• Smart Image Discovery: Automatically finds the cleanest, highest-resolution product photography on the page. Pick your favorite shot from up to 6 candidates.
• AI Vision Pipeline: The moment you save a piece, our server-side vision AI tags its category (clothes, shoes & accessories, home & living, tools & tech, curios), detects the brand, extracts the color palette, and tags aesthetic vibes (minimalist, vintage, cottagecore, industrial, etc.).
• Smart Auto-Grouping: Pieces are automatically organized into dynamic smart boards based on aesthetics, categories, and brands.
• Image-First, Minimalist Design: No aggressive prices, noisy banner ads, or cluttered titles by default. Your board looks like a curated museum of things you love on tactile sand canvas.
• Keyboard Shortcut: Press Alt+Shift+S (or Mac Ctrl+Shift+S) on any product page for instant capture.
• Right-Click Shortcut: Right-click any image or page to "Save to crumbs".

HOW TO USE
1. Install the extension.
2. Click the crumbs icon and click "Connect crumbs" to securely link your account.
3. Browse the web. When you see something you love, tap the icon or press Alt+Shift+S.
4. Your piece immediately appears on your board at crumbsbypixel.vercel.app!
```

## 4. Permission Justifications (for Chrome Web Store review)

- **`activeTab`**:  
  *Justification:* Needed only when the user explicitly clicks the extension action or presses the quick-capture keyboard shortcut, to inspect the active product page and extract candidate product photography and page titles.
- **`storage`**:  
  *Justification:* Used exclusively to store the user's private capture token and server URL locally in Chrome sync storage, authenticating capture requests without storing credentials.
- **`contextMenus`**:  
  *Justification:* Allows users to right-click on any image or product link and select "Save to crumbs".
- **`scripting`**:  
  *Justification:* Used as a fallback to execute DOM product image detection on dynamic single-page application storefronts when requested by the user.
- **`host_permissions` (`https://crumbsbypixel.vercel.app/*`, `http://localhost:3000/*`)**:  
  *Justification:* Required to communicate with the crumbs capture API endpoint (`/api/capture`) and securely receive the capture token during the one-click authentication handshake on `/connect`.
